//Q4 Write down the steps (as an algorithm, using simple numbered instructions) for ordering food on Swiggy — from opening the app to completing payment.

Algorithm:

1. Start.
2. Open the Swiggy app.
3. Log in to your account (if required).
4. Select or confirm your delivery location.
5. Search for a restaurant.
6. Open the restaurant menu.
7. Select the food items you want.
8. Add the items to the cart.
9. Review the cart and click Proceed to Checkout.
10. Apply a coupon (optional).
11. Choose the payment method.
12. Complete the payment.
13. Wait for the order confirmation.
14. Track the delivery status.
15. Receive the food.
16. End.
