About Aria

Starting her journey in classic piano training, Aria Vance evolved her sound by merging analog synthesizers with emotional vocal hooks. Over the last four years, her self-produced tracks have accrued millions of streams globally.

When not in her Brooklyn recording space, Aria tours intimate indie venues and works on film compositions.
